# Twingate Support Options

## Summary
Twingate provides tiered support based on subscription plan. Business and Enterprise plans receive technical break-fix support; Starter, Teams, and trial accounts are limited to self-service resources. Multiple channels exist depending on account type and need.

## Key Information

- **Technical Support** (break-fix) is only available for **Business** and **Enterprise** plan subscribers
- **Starter, Teams, and trial Business accounts** receive no direct product support or troubleshooting assistance
- Community support via Subreddit is available to all users regardless of plan

## Support Channels by Use Case

| Need | Channel |
|------|---------|
| General documentation | [Twingate Docs](https://www.twingate.com/docs) |
| Self-service help articles | Twingate Help Center |
| Community discussion/troubleshooting | Twingate Subreddit |
| Billing/subscription questions | Subscription Management page |
| Break-fix technical issues | Technical Support Services (Business/Enterprise only) |
| Pre-sales inquiries | Contact sales representative |
| Self-serve trial onboarding | onboarding@twingate.com |

## Prerequisites

- **Technical Support access requires**: Active Business or Enterprise plan subscription
- Pre-sales support requires a sales representative or self-serve trial status

## Gotchas

- Trial **Business** accounts do **not** qualify for Technical Support despite the plan name — only paid Business/Enterprise subscriptions qualify
- No troubleshooting assistance is provided for Starter or Teams plans; community Subreddit is the recommended fallback
- Pre-sales customers with a sales rep should route all Support Requests through that rep (not open separate tickets)
