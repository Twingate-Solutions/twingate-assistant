# Twingate Trust Center

## Page Title
Twingate Trust Center

## Summary
The Trust Center is a centralized hub for Twingate's security, privacy, legal, and compliance documentation. It provides transparency into Twingate's trustworthiness posture for customers evaluating or auditing the platform. No configuration or implementation steps are involved.

## Key Information

- **Security & Reliability**
  - Security information documentation available
  - Service status page for uptime/reliability monitoring
  - Responsible Disclosure Policy for vulnerability reporting

- **Privacy**
  - Privacy Policy
  - California Supplemental Privacy Notice (CCPA)

- **Legal**
  - Customer Agreement
  - User Terms of Service
  - Legal Center (broader legal documentation)

- **Compliance Certifications & Standards**
  - SOC 2 Report
  - GDPR Compliance
  - HIPAA Compliance
  - PCI DSS Compliance
  - DORA Compliance
  - EU Digital Services Act

- **Additional Resources**
  - Twingate Inc. Form W-9 (for vendor/procurement use)
  - Twingate & Customer Data documentation
  - Twingate Trust Portal (external portal)

## Prerequisites
None — this is a reference/index page for compliance and trust documentation.

## Step-by-Step
Not applicable — this page is an index of resources, not a configuration guide.

## Configuration Values
None.

## Gotchas
- Compliance certifications not listed (e.g., FedRAMP, ISO 27001) are not guaranteed to exist; contact Twingate directly for unlisted standards.
- SOC 2 Report likely requires an NDA or direct request — not publicly downloadable from this page.
