# GDPR Compliance

## Page Title
GDPR Compliance

## Summary
Twingate complies with GDPR in both data controller and data processor capacities. For customers subject to GDPR, Twingate provides a Data Processing Addendum (DPA) and uses Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for EU-to-US data transfers.

## Key Information
- Twingate acts as both a **data controller** (own operations) and **data processor** (on behalf of customers)
- As controller: maintains privacy notices, data subject rights procedures, security measures, and third-party processor oversight
- As processor: collects limited personal data from customers; commitments defined in Customer Agreement
- GDPR compliance is treated as a continuous program, not a one-time certification

## Data Transfer Mechanisms
- **EU → US transfers**: Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
- **UK → US transfers**: UK's International Data Transfer Addendum (UK GDPR compliance)

## For Customers Needing GDPR Compliance
- A **Data Processing Addendum (DPA)** is included in customer agreements
- Review the Customer Agreement for data handling commitments
- See [Twingate & Customer Data](https://www.twingate.com/docs/twingate-and-customer-data) for specifics on data types processed

## Gotchas
- Twingate is a US-based company — cross-border transfer mechanisms (SCCs, UK Addendum) are required for EU/UK data flows
- The DPA is part of the standard customer agreement, not a separate negotiated document by default
