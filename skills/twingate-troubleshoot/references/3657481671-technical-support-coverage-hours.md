# Technical Support Coverage Hours

## Page Title
Technical Support Coverage Hours

## Summary
Twingate offers two tiers of technical support with different coverage windows. Both tiers exclude U.S. company holidays. Actual hours may differ if specified in a customer's Support Services Agreement.

## Key Information
- **Priority Support**: Monday–Friday, 8:00am–5:00pm Pacific Time
- **Premier Support**: Sunday 2:00pm PT – Friday 6:00pm PT
- No support coverage on U.S. company holidays for either tier
- Hours can be overridden by individual Support Services Agreements

## Gotchas
- Holiday schedule is only published through January 1, 2027
- Premier Support does **not** cover weekends fully — coverage starts Sunday 2:00pm PT (not Sunday midnight)
- Premier Support ends Friday 6:00pm PT, leaving Saturday entirely uncovered
- Default hours apply unless a Support Services Agreement explicitly states otherwise
