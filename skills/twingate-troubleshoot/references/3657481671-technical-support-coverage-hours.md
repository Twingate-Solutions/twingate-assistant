# Technical Support Coverage Hours

## Page Title
Technical Support Coverage Hours

## Summary
Twingate offers two tiers of technical support with defined coverage windows. Both tiers exclude U.S. company holidays. Actual hours may differ if specified in a customer's Support Services Agreement.

## Key Information

- **Priority Support**: Monday–Friday, 8:00am–5:00pm Pacific Time
- **Premier Support**: Sunday 2:00pm PT – Friday 6:00pm PT
- All support tiers exclude U.S. company holidays
- Hours can be overridden by individual Support Services Agreements

## Gotchas

- Holiday schedule is only published through January 1, 2027
- Premier Support does **not** cover weekends fully — coverage starts Sunday 2:00pm PT, not Sunday midnight
- Priority Support ends at 5:00pm PT Friday; no weekend coverage
- Check your Support Services Agreement — contractual terms override the published hours
