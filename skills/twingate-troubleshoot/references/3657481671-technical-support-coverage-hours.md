# Technical Support Coverage Hours

## Page Title
Technical Support Coverage Hours

## Summary
Twingate offers two tiers of technical support with different coverage windows. Both tiers exclude U.S. company holidays. Actual hours may vary if your Support Services Agreement specifies otherwise.

## Key Information

**Support Tiers:**
- **Priority Support:** Monday–Friday, 8:00am–5:00pm Pacific Time
- **Premier Support:** Sunday 2:00pm PT – Friday 6:00pm PT

**2027:**
- Jan 1 (Fri) – New Year's Day

## Gotchas
- No support coverage on any listed U.S. company holidays regardless of support tier
- Premier Support does **not** cover Saturday or Friday after 6:00pm PT
- Hours listed are defaults; your Support Services Agreement may specify different terms
- Holiday schedule is only published through January 1, 2027

## Prerequisites
- Determine your support tier (Priority vs. Premier) from your contract
- Check your Support Services Agreement for any custom coverage terms
