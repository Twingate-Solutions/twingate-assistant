# How to Delete a Twingate Network/Tenant

## Summary
Explains the process for permanently deleting a Twingate network tenant via the Admin Console. Requires downgrading to a free Starter plan before deletion is available. Enterprise/invoice accounts must contact their Twingate representative instead.

## Key Information
- Only applies to **Teams** or **Business** plan holders (self-serve)
- **Enterprise/invoice accounts** must contact their Twingate rep directly
- **End-users** cannot use this process — contact your Admin to remove your user account
- Delete option only appears after subscription downgrades to Starter (at end of billing period)

## Prerequisites
- Admin Console access
- Active subscription downgraded to Starter plan (or already on Starter)
- Subscription period must have lapsed before deletion option appears

## Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Downgrade Subscription (if on Teams or Business)
1. Downgrade to the free Starter plan (see *Downgrade your subscription* docs)
2. Downgrade takes effect at the **next billing period** — not immediately
3. Verify scheduled downgrade via *How do I check that my subscription will downgrade?*

### Step 2: Delete the Network
Once subscription shows as Starter:
1. Sign into the **Admin Console**
2. Click **Settings**
3. Click **Billing**
4. Scroll to bottom → locate **Delete Network Account** section
5. Click **Delete**
6. Follow confirmation prompts

## Gotchas
- **Delete Network Account section is hidden** while any active paid subscription exists — must wait until billing period ends
- Downgrade is **not immediate**; plan remains active until end of current billing cycle
- This deletes the entire **network/tenant**, not an individual user account
- Enterprise and invoice customers have **no self-serve deletion path**
