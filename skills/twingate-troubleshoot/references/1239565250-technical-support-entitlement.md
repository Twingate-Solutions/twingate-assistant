# Technical Support Entitlement

## Page Title
Technical Support Entitlement

## Summary
Twingate offers tiered support based on subscription plan. Break/fix ticketed support is available for Teams, Business, and Enterprise plans only. Community support via Reddit is available to all plans.

## Key Information

- **Community Support (Subreddit):** Available to all plans (Starter, Home, Teams, Business, Enterprise)
- **Ticketed Support:** Teams, Business, Enterprise only
- **End users should not submit tickets** — they must go through their Twingate Admin or IT team
- **Pre-sales/trial support** is routed through sales reps or the onboarding team

## Support Channel Eligibility Matrix

| Channel | Enterprise | Business | Teams | Home | Starter | End Users |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Community (Reddit) | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ⚠️ (use Admin) |
| Ticketed Support | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Pre-Sales/Trial | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |

## Prerequisites
- Must be a Twingate Admin to submit ticketed support requests
- Must be on Teams, Business, or Enterprise plan for ticket access

## Contact Points
- **Ticketed support:** Via Twingate support portal (Teams/Business/Enterprise)
- **Trial/onboarding (no sales rep):** `onboarding@twingate.com`
- **Community:** [Twingate Subreddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/twingate)
- **Pre-sales POC:** Contact assigned sales representative

## Gotchas
- Home and Starter plans have **no ticketed support** — Reddit is the only official channel
- End users (non-admins) are explicitly excluded from ticketed support regardless of plan
- Self-serve trial customers without a sales rep must use email or Reddit — there is no ticket portal access for trials
