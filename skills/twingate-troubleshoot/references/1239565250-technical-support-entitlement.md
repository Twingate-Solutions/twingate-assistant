# Technical Support Entitlement

## Page Title
Technical Support Entitlement

## Summary
Twingate offers tiered support based on subscription plan. Community support via Reddit is available to all plans; ticketed break/fix support is restricted to Teams, Business, and Enterprise subscribers. End users should always route issues through their Twingate admins rather than contacting Twingate directly.

## Key Information

- **Community Support (Subreddit):** Available to all plans (Starter, Home, Teams, Business, Enterprise)
- **Ticketed Support:** Teams, Business, Enterprise only
- **End users are explicitly excluded** from ticketed support — must go through their admin/IT team
- **Home and Starter plans** have no ticketed support entitlement

## Support Channel Eligibility Matrix

| Channel | Eligible Plans |
|---|---|
| Community (Subreddit) | All plans |
| Ticketed (break/fix) | Teams, Business, Enterprise |
| Pre-sales/Trial support | Business/Enterprise POC; Teams/Business trials |

## Pre-Sales / Trial Support

- Contact your **sales representative** to centralize support requests
- Self-serve trial customers (no sales rep): email **onboarding@twingate.com** or post on the Subreddit
- Home and Starter trials: **not eligible** for pre-sales support

## Gotchas

- End users contacting Twingate support directly will be redirected — only admins/IT should submit tickets
- Home and Starter plan users have **no ticketed support path**; community forum is the only option
- Trial eligibility for support is limited to Teams, Business, and Enterprise tiers only
